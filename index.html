2027 Tentative BudgetWhy did my property taxes change?What changed in the budget?Who gets paid the most?How are Town property taxes used?What could happen in 2027?
The Town’s 2027 Tentative Budget is out. It proposes a town-wide tax levy of $62,882,202, up 2.79% from 2026, for the General Fund, Highway and Street Lighting. With the special districts, the levy is $67,159,863, up 2.78% from 2026 and $1,807,211 less than this site’s forecast of $68,967,074. The Supervisor’s letter says every taxing district is within the tax cap limit of 2.79%, and no district’s levy rises by more than that. See how it compares →

Riverhead Budget Live is an independent project. It is not run, approved, or endorsed by the Town of Riverhead. The site organizes public information and provides analysis; it is not a substitute for the Town’s official records. Check the linked source documents before relying on an important figure.
