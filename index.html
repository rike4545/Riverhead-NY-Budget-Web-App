The Town’s 2027 Preliminary Budget is out.

The Board’s six amendments on October 20 turned the Supervisor’s Tentative into it. Its town-wide levy is $62,238,344, up 1.73% from 2026, against $62,882,202, up 2.79%, in the Tentative. The General Fund levy is $53,784,658, $552,292 lower. It is still a proposal. The public hearing was held on November 5, 2026, and the Board must adopt a budget by November 20; if it does not, the Preliminary takes effect.

The Tentative, as filed in September: It proposes a town-wide tax levy of $62,882,202, up 2.79% from 2026, for the General Fund, Highway and Street Lighting. With the special districts, the levy is $67,159,863, up 2.78% from 2026 and $1,807,211 less than this site’s forecast of $68,967,074. The Supervisor’s letter says every taxing district is within the tax cap limit of 2.79%, and no district’s levy rises by more than that.
