How the current Town Board was elected

How many actual votes put each current Riverhead Town Board member in office — and how that compares to the town's total population and its registered voters. A low share isn't an accusation; it's the normal reality of low-turnout local elections, and a useful reminder of how few votes decide who controls the Town's budget.

A neighbor made this to make the Town's finances easier to follow. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the Town of Riverhead, and figures can carry parsing errors — so double-check anything important against the official documents before you rely on it.Last refreshed July 27, 2026 · new meeting minutes and financial-report documents are fetched and parsed automatically every week.

💡How to read this
Each card leads with the raw votes that won the seat, then measures them two ways: against the registered voters (people who could have voted) and against the whole population (which also counts kids and others who can't vote). Neither is a turnout rate — together they just show how small a slice of the town actually chose the people who now control its budget. Low shares are normal for off-year local elections.
