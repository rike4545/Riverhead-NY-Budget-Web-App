Outlier Watch

Riverhead's 19 town funds, checked year over year against their own budget history. This flags appropriation swings large enough to be worth a second look — not proof of a problem, but a place to start asking questions.

💡In plain English
Across 114 fund-year comparisons since 2020, 6 clear the bar for a flag.
  • ›What counts as an outlier: a fund whose appropriation moved at least 20% AND at least $100,000 from one year's adopted budget to the next.
  • ›Why two thresholds: percent alone flags small funds swinging on a tiny base (a $15,000 fund going to $25,000 is "+67%" but immaterial); dollars alone would flag the General Fund almost every year simply because it is the biggest fund.
  • ›What it does not mean: a flag is not an accusation. Big swings often have ordinary explanations — a bond payoff, a new contract, a one-time grant. Treat this as a starting point for a question at a Town Board meeting, not a conclusion.
