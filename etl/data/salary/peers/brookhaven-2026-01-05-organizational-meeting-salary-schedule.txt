             Title: Establishment of Salary Schedule for Town Employees

Department: Law Department                            Permissive                No
                                                      Referendum:
Sponsors:    Daniel Panico, Supervisor                SEQRA Required:           No

Financial Impact
Adequate 2026 budget for Townwide employee compensation and benefits exists to
fund the 2026 Salary Schedule.

Reason
Establishment of Salary Schedule for Town Employees

Body
BE IT HEREBY RESOLVED by the Town Board of the Town of Brookhaven that,
except as otherwise set forth therein, the attached listed employees shall receive
salaries as indicated herein effective January 1, 2026, and be it further

RESOLVED, that the Commissioner of Finance shall amend, where required, the 2026
budget staffing schedule to accurately reflect the attached schedule.
