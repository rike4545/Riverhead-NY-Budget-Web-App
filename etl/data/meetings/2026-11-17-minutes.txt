Council Member Merrifield stated the Anti-Biased task force meeting on January 
11th,2027, will be coordinated with Suffolk County Hate Crimes Unit. The meeting 
will begin at 5:30 at the Town Hall. All are encouraged to attend.  
Council Member Waski stated the BID Merry Market at town hall on December 5th, 
3pm-8pm- Lions Club parade will begin at 5:00 and end at town hall — Bon Fire 
and music and tree lighting will take place. 
Council Member Merrifield thanked Warren McKnight for his letter he wrote to the 
board during the Thanksgiving season. 
The supervisor reminded the public that Town Hall would be closed on 
Thanksgiving the 26th and Friday the 27th. The Recreation Department is 
conducting a Friends' Giving at the George Young Community Center on 
November 21st from 6:30 to 8:00. Also, the Santa Mailbox will be in front of Town 
Hall for special deliveries to the North Pole.

VII.  Public Hearings 
 ● 6:00 PM Public Hearing - Amend Chapter 301, Art. X - Hamlet Center  
The Public Hearing opened at 6:11 PM. Town Planner Greg Bergman gave an 
overview of the code amendment to all the area zoning districts with regard to off-
street parking. Presented a study conducted as to the utilization of parking and the 
availability of additional uses within large paved parking lots. No public comments 
were received. The hearing was closed and held open for 10 days till November 
27th for written comments.  
 ● 6:00 PM Public Hearing - Amend Chapter 301, Art.XI - Village Center  
 ● 6:00 PM Public Hearing - Amend Chapter 301, Art XIX - Business CR (Rural 
Neighborhood Business)  
 ● 6:00 PM Public Hearing - Amend Chapter 301;Art, XLV - Supplementary Use 
Regulations  
 ● 6:00 PM Public Hearing - Amend Chapter 301, Art. XVIII - Business Center  
 ● 6:15 PM Public Hearing - Amend Chapter 291 - Water District  
Town Attorney Erik Howard gave an overview of fine increases in water tampering. 
No public comment. The hearing was closed and open for ten days till November 
27th for written comment.  
 ● 6:20 PM Public Hearing - Amend Chapter 289 - Vehicles, Traffic and Parking 
Regulations  
Town Attorney Erik Howard gave an explanation of the amendment to add an 
additional handicap parking stall along the north side of second street. No public 
comment. The hearing was closed and held open for ten days until November 27th 
for written comment.

X.  Open Comments from the Public 
Warren McKnight inquired about the condition of the MTA's Railroad Station 
building and if that entity addressed the issues. Councilmember Waski stated they 
are working and coordinating efforts to address that issue in anticipation of the 
urban renewal plan for that area of downtown.  
Colin Tooker — Stated that the Town has not provided Desmond Wong with 
answers to policies concerning immigration. Stated the Hispanic Committee 
directed him to share his concerns with the town board as they were focused more 
on their mission. Anti-Bias task force responded that they are case specific and 
don't create policy. 
Sara Reynolds-OLA advocate, urging the board to consider adopting their policy.  
Kevin Shea — Inquired about the propane leak on Kromer Ave, Fire Marshall, and 
coordinated efforts with Suffolk County recognized and thanked by the Supervisor. 
Again alluded to a lawsuit he intends to file against social media posting, and 
asked if Council Member Waski would like to respond.
