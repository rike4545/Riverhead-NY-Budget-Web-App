CALVERTON, NEW YORK  
 
WHEREAS, a petition has been filed by the owner/developer of 1001 Scott Avenue in Calverton, of an 
approximately 10.05 acre site located on the west side of Scott Avenue with the Calverton Enterprise Park 
Industrial Subdivision and designated as District 0600, Section 135.10, Block 01, Lot 001 on the Suffolk County Tax 
Map (hereinafter the “Development”). The Development is proposed to be improved with a single-story industrial 
warehouse consisting of approximately 70,445 square feet of space and the developer is requesting that the 
Development be served by the Riverhead Water District; and WHEREAS, the proposed facility is located within the 
existing boundaries of the Riverhead Water District. The proposed Development building is situated where it will 
be in direct conflict with the existing facilities of the Water District and therefore this conflicted water main shall be 
relocated; and WHEREAS, a map and plan dated September, 2026 has been prepared by H2M, consulting 
engineers to the Riverhead Water District, detailing the necessary measures and costs associated with extending 
the Riverhead Water District facilities to service the Development; and WHEREAS, the map and plan are available 
for review and inspection at the Office of the Riverhead Town Clerk, 4 West Second Street, Riverhead, New York, 
during normal business hours; and WHEREAS, said map and plan call for an extension of the existing water facilities 
will be required to provide service to the Development. The Water District will require the owner to install 
approximately 450 feet of twelve-inch cement lined ductile iron water main along Scott Avenue and within an 
existing Utility Easement previously dedicated to the Town of Riverhead. This new water main will maintain the 
existing fire flow within the industrial subdivision. To provide services to the development, domestic and fire 
sprinkler services will connect to an existing water main on Scott Avenue. WHEREAS, the cost to extend these 
proposed facilities shall be solely the responsibility of owner. The estimated project cost to implement the 
replacement of water main to serve the development is $347,000.00. These costs also include engineering, 
permitting, construction observation, legal, administration and contingencies, excluding Water District Key Money 
fees; and WHEREAS, as stated above, all costs associated with this lateral shall be borne by the petitioner and the 
petitioner will be required to pay key money fees for water usage. The industrial space will be subject to Key 
Money fees based on a projected water usage of 34,600 gallons per day. Key Money Fees are $9.10 per gallon for a 
total assessed key money fee of $27,045.00. In addition, the properties will be taxed the standard Water District 
tax rate; and WHEREAS, the extension of the existing Water District facilities, pursuant to 6 NYCRR Part 617.5, this 
Development is considered a Type II Action and therefore is not subject to no further review, and WHEREAS, the 
Town Board is required to call a public hearing on the adoption of a final order concerning the petition. NOW, 
THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Town Clerk be and is hereby authorized to publish and post a Notice of 
Public Hearing to be held on the 5th day of November, 2026 at 2:00 p.m. at the Riverhead Town Hall, 4 West 
Second Street, Riverhead, New York, to hear all interested persons with regard to the adoption of a final order 
concerning the petition for water main extension as described in the preambles hereto to provide water service to 
the proposed development at 1001 Scott Avenue located at District 0600, Section 135.10, Block 01, Lot 001 on the 
Suffolk County Tax Map, Calverton, New York, at the sole cost of the developer and at no cost to the Riverhead 
Water District and the payment of key money pursuant to the Riverhead Town Code; and, be it further, RESOLVED, 
that the Town Clerk be and is hereby authorized to publish and post a copy of this resolution in full in the October 
15, 2026 edition of The News Review; and be it further, RESOLVED, that the Town Clerk is hereby directed to

Chapter 279. Taxation 
Article III. Veterans Exemption 
§ 279-7A. Property exempt from taxation. 
A.  1.   
 
If the ratio between the exemption granted under § 458 of the Real Property Tax Law and 
the total assessed value of the real property for which such exemption has been granted 
increased or decreased due only to the 1979 full assessment by the Town of Riverhead, 
the amount of the exemption heretofore or hereafter granted shall be increased or 
decreased in such subsequent year in the same proportion as the total assessed value 
had been increased or decreased in 1979. Such adjustment shall be made by the 
Assessors in the manner provided in § 458 of the Real Property Tax Law, and no 
application therefor need be filed by or on behalf of any owner of any eligible property. 
B. 2.   
Where property which is owned by a veteran and receiving any exemption pursuant to 
§ 458 of the Real Property Tax Law is sold and other real property is acquired to replace 
the formerly owned property, the exempt amount granted on the formerly owned property 
pursuant to this subsection and as expressed in a percentage shall be granted on the 
property acquired to replace the formerly owned property, provided that the newly 
acquired property is in the same assessing unit, and provided, further, that the exemption 
granted on the newly acquired property shall not exceed the exemption on the formerly 
owned property as expressed in a dollar amount. 
 
§ 279-7B. (1). Alternate Tax Exemption for Permanently and Totally Disabled Veterans. 
This Local Law shall be a new Section under Chapter 279  of the Town Code of the Town 
of Riverhead titled “Tax Exemption for Permanently and Totally Disabled Veterans.” 
 
§ 279-7B (2) Purpose and Findings. 
 
It is the purpose of this Local Law to exercise the authority granted to local taxing 
jurisdictions by Real Property Tax Law Section 458-a(11) which authorizes a Veterans 
Alternative Exemption. The Town Board of the Town of Riverhead finds and hereby 
determines that New York Real Property Tax Law Section 458-a(11) makes available a 
tax exemption for permanently and totally disabled veterans as a result of military service 
at the option of the local municipality. The Town Board of the Town of Riverhead further 
finds and determines that the Town of Riverhead should recognize the extraordinary 
service and sacrifice of such Veterans and the life-long hardships caused by such service 
and sacrifice.  Accordingly, the Town Board finds that exercising said option and 
Page 8 of 120
authorizing said tax exemption to furthers protections for Veterans residing within the 
Town of Riverhead. 
 
§ 279-7B(3) Grant of Property Tax Exemption for Permanently and Totally Disabled 
Veterans. 
 
Chapter 279 of the Town Code for the Town of Riverhead, “Taxation” is amended to add 
Tax Exemption for Permanently and Totally Disabled Veterans” as set forth herein. 
 
§279-7 B(4) (a) The Real Property Tax Law has been amended by the addition of a §458-
a(11), which permits the Town of Riverhead to grant a full tax exemption for the primary 
residence of any seriously disabled veteran. Said exemption is a full exemption from 
taxation and special district charges, assessments and special ad valorem levies and 
shall be hereby granted, upon application therefor and subject to the requirements set 
forth herein below: 
  
(i) Eligibility.   
 
Pursuant to §458-a(11) of the New York Real Property Tax Law, such exemption shall 
be granted provided that a veteran:  
  
A. must have been discharged or released from active military, naval, space or air 
service, including army and air national guard service pursuant to federal orders 
under title 10 of the United States code, under honorable conditions; or  
 
B. must have a qualifying condition, as defined by section one of the veterans’ 
services law, and must have received a discharge other than bad conduct or 
dishonorable from such service; or  
 
C. must be a discharged LGBT veteran, as defined in section one of the veterans’ 
services law, and must have received a discharge other than bad conduct or 
dishonorable from such service; and  
 
D. must be considered by the United States department of veterans affairs to be 
permanently and totally disabled as a result of military service, as evidenced by a 
letter, official form, or other document sent 6.2 to such veteran from such 
department that specifically states such veteran is considered to be permanently 
and totally disabled as a result of such service. 
 
(ii)    Application. 
 
Application for such exemption shall be filed with the assessor of the Town of 
Riverhead on or before the taxable status date on a form as prescribed by the 
Commissioner of Tax and Finance.

ALJ/Professional Services Agreement 
 
This Agreement made the ___ day of November, 2026 between the Town of Riverhead, a 
municipal corporation organized and existing under the laws of New York, with its office located 
at 4 West Second Street, Riverhead, New York, 11901 (hereinafter referred to as the “TOWN”), 
and Hon. Lori M. Hulse (hereinafter referred to as “ALJ”). 
In consideration of the mutual promises herein contained, Town Riverhead and ALJ agree as 
follows: 
1. SCOPE OF SERVICES 
During the term of this Agreement, the ALJ shall adjudicate bus stop-arm camera violations and 
issue final administrative determinations subject to CPLR Article 78 review. Proceedings shall be 
conducted pursuant to applicable provisions of the New York Vehicle and Traffic Law and Town 
Code. 
 
2. TERM OF AGREEMENT 
This Agreement commenced on April 15, 2026 and shall terminate on December 31, 2028 or when 
the Stop-Arm Program is no longer in effect, whichever first occurs. 
 
3. PAYMENT 
For these services, the Town of Riverhead will pay ALJ an annual fee in the amount not to exceed 
$11,250.00 for all services described above and to be provided under this Agreement, except that 
the total payable year 2026 shall be not greater than $7,500.00. 
 
4. RIGHTS TO DOCUMENTS OR DATA 
All information and data, regardless of form, generated in the performance of, or delivered under, 
this Agreement, as well as any information provided to ALJ by Town, shall be and remain the sole 
property of Town. ALJ shall keep all such information and data in confidence and not disclose or 
use it for any purpose other than in performing this Agreement, except with Town's prior written 
approval. In the event that the legal right in any data and information generated in the performance 
of this Agreement does not vest in Town by law, ALJ hereby agrees to assign and assigns to Town 
such legal rights in all such data and information. These obligations shall survive the termination 
of this Agreement. 
 
5. HEARINGS 
Hearings may be conducted in person. The ALJ shall administer oaths, control proceedings, and 
rule on admissibility. 
 
6. EVIDENCE 
Sworn certificates, camera images, video recordings, and business records shall constitute prima 
facie evidence if reliable.

influencing or attempting to influence any such persons in connection with the award, extension, 
continuation, renewal, amendment or modification of any contract or agreement.

Town. ALJ further represents that it will not engage in any activity which presents a conflict of 
interest in light of its relationship with Town.

Article I. Connections, Maintenance and Rates 
§ 291-20 Injury to district installations. 
Any willful injury to any installation provided by the Water District constitutes a 
misdemeanor and shall be punishable by a penalty not exceeding $100. as follows: 
 
A. By a fine of not less than $2500.00 and not exceeding $5,000.00 or by 
imprisonment for a period not to exceed 15 days, or both, for conviction of a first 
offense. 
B.  By a fine of not less than $5000.00 nor more than $7500.00 or by imprisonment 
for a period not to exceed 15 days, or both, for conviction of the second of two 
offenses, both of which were committed within a period of five years. 
C. By a fine of not less than $7500.00 nor more than $10,000.00 or by imprisonment 
for a period not to exceed 15 days, or both, for conviction of the third or subsequent 
offenses of a series of offenses, all of which were committed within a period of five 
years. 
D.  Each days continued violation shall constitute a separate and additional violation.  
E. Each and every aforementioned fine shall be doubled for violations committed by 
nonresidents and/or commercial entities. 
F. Conviction pursuant to this section shall be cumulative and additional to any fee or 
charge imposed by this Chapter 291, including restitution for any damage done to 
district facilities.

Re: Proposed Contingency and Tax Stabilization Reserve and Updated Fund Balance 
Target Request 
Dear Supervisor Halpin: 
I write to respectfully request that the Town Board take two actions in connection with the 
adoption of the 2027 budget. First, I ask that the Board adopt the draft resolution I 
submitted on June 17, 2026, a copy of which is enclosed. That resolution would establish a 
Contingency and Tax Stabilization Reserve under General Municipal Law S 6-e and set a 
General Fund balance target of 25 percent of budgeted expenditures. Second, I ask that the 
Preliminary Budget state the General Fund balance expected to remain after the 2027 
appropriations, as Town Law S 107 requires. 
The Town's General Fund is in a strong position. According to the audited financial statements 
the Board accepted on September 1, 2026, the unassigned General Fund balance at 
December 31, 2025 was $28,829,513, or 41.7 percent of the 2026 General Fund budget. The 
Town's fund balance policy, adopted by Resolution 918 of 2011, calls for a minimum of 15 
percent. 
That policy, however, establishes only a minimum. It does not state a target level, the 
purposes for which a balance above the minimum should be used, or how the balance is to 
be restored after it is drawn down. In practice, the balance has continued to grow: the 2025 
budget anticipated the use of $1 , 750,000 of the General Fund balance, and the balance 
instead increased by $4,899,127. 
State law also addresses the size of the remaining balance. Town Law S 107 provides that 
the unappropriated fund balance remaining after the budget "shall not exceed a reasonable 
amount," and requires the Preliminary Budget to set out the estimated fund balances. The 
2027 Tentative Budget reports a General Fund balance of $33,303,051 at December 31, 
2026, the same figure as one year earlier, and does not show the amount remaining after 
the 2027 appropriation. The 2027 budgets of the Towns of Brookhaven, Southold and 
Southampton each present that figure. 
Page 109 of 120
A Contingency and Tax Stabilization Reserve would give a defined portion of the Town's 
savings a lawful and transparent purpose. Under General Municipal Law S 6-e: 
1. the reserve may hold up to 10 percent of the General Fund and Highway Fund budgets, 
approximately $8,030,040 based on the 2027 Tentative Budget; 
 
2. it may be used to offset unanticipated revenue losses or expenditures, or to limit a 
projected increase in the tax levy to 2.5 percent; 
3. any appropriation from it requires the recommendation of the Supervisor and the 
approval of at least two-thirds of the Town Board; and 
4. Its establishment is by Board resolution, subject to permissive referendum. 
The Town of Southampton maintains such a reserve as part of its fund balance policy, and 
the Town of Brookhaven's policy establishes a 25 percent General Fund minimum. Funding 
the reserve at its full limit would leave Riverhead's General Fund well above its current 15 
percent minimum. The proposal would not increase taxes or reduce services; it would 
establish clear rules for how the Town's accumulated savings are held and used. 
I would welcome the opportunity to meet with you or the Financial Administrator to discuss 
this proposal. My previous attempts, over the phone, to schedule a meeting with your office 
have not been successful. 
Thank you for your consideration. 
Respectfully,

TOWN OF RIVERHEAD DRAFT 
RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING A CONTINGENCY AND TAX STABILIZATION 
RESERVE 
FUND, ALSO REFERRED TO AS A RAINY DAY FUND, AND ADOPTING A 
BROOKHAVEN STYLE GENERAL FUND RESERVE POLICY 
WHEREAS, the Town Board of the Town of Riverhead recognizes the importance of long-term 
fiscal planning, stable tax rates, and responsible management of surplus funds; and 
WHEREAS, the Town has experienced prior increases in the real property tax levy, and the 
Town Board finds that a formal reserve mechanism would help reduce the risk of sharp year-
toyear tax impacts on residents and taxpayers; and 
WHEREAS, New York General Municipal Law S 6-e authorizes towns to establish a contingency 
and tax stabilization reserve fund by resolution, subject to permissive referendum; and 
WHEREAS, such a fund is commonly understood as a "rainy day fund" because it allows a 
municipality to reserve funds for limited, legally authorized purposes, including 
unanticipated revenue losses, unanticipated expenditures, and stabilization of certain 
future tax levy increases; and 
WHEREAS, the Town Board desires to model the Town of Riverhead's reserve policy on fiscally 
prudent practices used by the Town of Brookhaven, including the maintenance of a minimum 
General Fund reserve equal to a defined percentage of budgeted expenditures; and 
WHEREAS, the Town of Brookhaven has maintained a fund balance policy requiring a 
minimum General Fund balance, exclusive of nonspendable fund balance, of no less than 
twenty-five percent (25%) of total budgeted General Fund expenditures; and 
WHEREAS, the Town Board finds that adopting a similar twenty-five percent (25%) General 
Fund reserve target would strengthen Riverhead's fiscal position, improve long-term 
planning, stabilize taxes, and reduce reliance on sudden tax levy increases or one-time 
budget actions; and 
WHEREAS, the Town Board further finds that the creation of a tax stabilization reserve fund should 
be part of a broader four-tier fiscal strategy that includes taxpayer relief, prudent capital 
investment, workforce and personnel planning, and long-term tax stabilization; and 
Page 111 of 120
WHEREAS, the Town Board finds that establishing such a fund would further the Town's 
stated commitment to good fiscal stewardship by ensuring that surplus funds are managed 
through a disciplined, transparent, and legally authorized reserve structure; 
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT 
RESOLVED, that the Town Board of the Town of Riverhead hereby establishes a fund 
to be known as the Town of Riverhead Contingency and Tax Stabilization Reserve 
Fund, also referred to as the Town of Riverhead Rainy Day Fund, pursuant to New 
York General Municipal Law S 6-e; and be it further 
RESOLVED, that the purpose of the Fund shall be to provide a fiscally responsible reserve 
for the stabilization of Town finances and real property taxes, including, where authorized 
by law, financing unanticipated revenue losses, financing unanticipated expenditures, and 
lessening or preventing qualifying projected increases in the real property tax levy; and be 
it further 
RESOLVED, that the Fund may be funded from such amounts as may be provided by 
budgetary appropriation, unappropriated unreserved fund balance from the eligible 
portion of the annual budget, and such revenues as are not otherwise required by law to 
be paid into another fund or account; and be it further 
RESOLVED, that no amount shall be appropriated into the Fund if such appropriation would cause 
the balance of the Fund to exceed the maximum amount permitted under General Municipal Law 
S 6-e, presently ten percent (10%) of the eligible portion of the annual budget; and be it further 
RESOLVED, that the Town Board hereby establishes a fiscal policy goal of maintaining General 
Fund reserves, exclusive of nonspendable fund balance, at no less than twenty-five percent 
(25%) of total budgeted General Fund expenditures, consistent with the reserve 
percentage maintained by the Town of Brookhaven; and be it further 
RESOLVED, that this twenty-five percent (25%) General Fund reserve target shall operate 
in addition to, and not in conflict with, the Town of Riverhead Contingency and Tax 
Stabilization Reserve Fund established pursuant to General Municipal Law S 6-e; and be 
it further 
RESOLVED, that if an emergency, unanticipated expenditure, unanticipated revenue loss, 
or other lawful appropriation causes the Town's General Fund reserves to fall below the 
twentyfive percent (25%) target, the Town Board shall, by resolution, identify the reason 
for such use and establish a plan to restore the reserve level over a reasonable period, 
preferably not exceeding three fiscal years; and be it further 
RESOLVED, that the Supervisor of the Town of Riverhead, as Chief Fiscal Officer, or such 
other officer having custody of Town funds, is hereby authorized and directed to deposit, 
invest, and secure the monies of the Fund in the manner provided by law, including General 
Municipal Law SS 10 and 11; and be it further 
Page 112 of 120
RESOLVED, that any interest earned or capital gains realized on monies deposited or invested 
in the Fund shall accrue to and become part of the Fund; and be it further 
RESOLVED, that the Fund may be combined in a bank account with other Town funds, 
provided that the separate identity of the Fund shall be maintained and the Fund shall be 
accounted for separately from all other Town funds; and be it further 
RESOLVED, that the Chief Fiscal Officer shall maintain accounting records for the Fund 
separate and apart from all other Town funds, including the source, date, and amount of 
each sum paid into the Fund; interest earned; capital gains or losses; the date, amount, and 
purpose of any appropriation from the Fund; and the assets of the Fund; and be it further 
RESOLVED, that the Town Supervisor, Budget Officer, Finance Administrator, or other 
appropriate fiscal officer shall annually report to the Town Board, as part of the tentative 
budget process, the Town's General Fund reserve percentage, the balance of the Contingency 
and Tax Stabilization Reserve Fund, and whether the Town remains in compliance with the 
reserve targets and statutory limits set forth herein; and be it further 
RESOLVED, that no expenditure, transfer, or appropriation shall be made from the Fund 
except in accordance with General Municipal Law S 6-e, including any required 
recommendation of the Town Supervisor and approval by the Town Board by the vote 
required by law; and be it further 
RESOLVED, that the Fund shall not be used as a general-purpose surplus account, nor shall 
it be used to avoid ordinary budget discipline, but shall instead be reserved for the limited 
purposes authorized by General Municipal Law S 6-e; and be it further 
RESOLVED, that in preparing each tentative budget, the Town shall review the balance of 
the Fund and apply any excess balance as required by General Municipal Law S 6-e; and be 
it further 
RESOLVED, that the members of the Town Board shall act as trustees of the monies in the 
Fund to the extent required by General Municipal Law S 6-e; and be it further 
RESOLVED, that this resolution shall be subject to permissive referendum as required by 
General Municipal Law S 6-e and applicable provisions of New York Town Law; and be it 
further 
RESOLVED, that the Town Clerk is hereby authorized and directed to publish and post such 
notice of adoption as may be required by law; and be it further 
RESOLVED, that this resolution shall take effect in accordance with applicable law.
