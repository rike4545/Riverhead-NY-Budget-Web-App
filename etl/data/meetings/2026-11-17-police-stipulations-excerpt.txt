                                    TOWN OF RIVERHEAD

                                        TB Resolution

           BUDGET ADJUSTMENT FOR RETIREMENT INCENTIVE SEVERANCE PAYOUTS

                             None offered the following resolution,
                                which was seconded by None

       WHEREAS, by Resolutions 2026-678 and 2026-680 adopted on July 7, 2026, the Town
Board did authorize and approve a stipulation with the CSEA, Local 1000, AFSCME, AFL-CIO,
Riverhead Unit of the Suffolk Local #852 and a stipulation with the Riverhead Town Police
Benevolent Association ("PBA") to offer a program incentive for voluntary retirement to certain
eligible members in order to generate savings for the Town in Fiscal Year 2027 and beyond;
and

       WHEREAS, three CSEA members and seven PBA members accepted this incentive, and
their retirement severance payouts require a budget transfer from the General Fund balance to
cover the associated one-time costs. Now, therefore be it

     RESOLVED, that the Town Board be, and is hereby authorized to establish the following
budget adjustments:

                                                              FROM               TO

A01-9999-000-00000-0 Appropriated Fund Balance          $1,387,034
     A01-3-3120-101-UNI-00000 Police Uni Personal Services                       $1,188,322
     A01-1-1110-101-NON-00000 Justice Court Personal Services                    $   37,180
     A01-1-1355-101-NON-00000 Assessors Personal Services                        $ 48,395
     A01-1-1680-101-NON-00000 IT Personal Services                               $ 14,569
     A01-9-9035-805-UNI-00000 FICA Police Uni                                    $ 90,907
     A01-9-9030-805-NON-00000 FICA Non-Uni                                       $    7,661

And be it further

       RESOLVED, that the Town Clerk is hereby authorized to forward a copy of this resolution
to finance@townofriverheadny.gov, and be it further

      RESOLVED, all Town Hall Departments may review and obtain a copy of this resolution
from the electronic storage device and, if needed, a certified copy of the same may be obtained
from the Office of the Town Clerk.
