TOWN OF RIVERHEAD 
 
TB Resolution 2025-643 
 
AUTHORIZES FUNDING APPLICATION TO NEW YORK STATE FOR CONSOLIDATED 
FUNDING APPLICATION (CFA) 2025 - PRO HOUSING PROGRAM 
 
Councilman Rothwell offered the following resolution,  
which was seconded by Councilman Kern 
 
          WHEREAS, the Town of Riverhead continues to advance the redevelopment of its 
Downtown which will enable it to transform into vibrant community where tomorrow’s workforce 
will want to live, work and raise families; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Empire State Development Corporation is accepting applications for the 
funding in its Consolidated Funding Application (CFA) 2025; and 
 
          WHEREAS, Georgica Green Ventures has acquired property located at 426 West Main 
Street where it wishes to construct a mixed-use, market-rate apartment building; and 
 
          WHEREAS, the Pro Housing program can provide funds to offset the cost of creating the 
necessary sewer, water and electrical infrastructure needed to construct the project as a pass-
through grant where the Town of Riverhead does not incur any costs or provide any matching 
funds; and 
 
          NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, that the Riverhead Town Board authorizes the 
submission of the Empire State Development Corp CFA funding application in the amount of up 
to $3.6M to New York State on or before July 31, 2025; and, be it further  
 
          RESOLVED, that the filing of the grant application is authorized, and the Supervisor is 
hereby authorized to sign any documents necessary in furtherance of this application as well as 
any contracts and related forms or documents that are required by the granting entity should the 
grant(s) be awarded; and, be it further 
 
          RESOLVED, that the Town Clerk forward copies of this resolution to the Community 
Development Department; and, be it further 
 
          RESOLVED, that all Town Hall Departments may review and obtain a copy of this 
resolution from the electronic storage device and, if needed, a certified copy of same may be 
obtained from the Office of the Town Clerk. 
  
 
 
THE VOTE
