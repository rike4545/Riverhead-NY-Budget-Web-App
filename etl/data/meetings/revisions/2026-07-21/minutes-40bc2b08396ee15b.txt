Warren McKnight — Thanked the supervisor and assembly member Giglio for

bathrooms.

and future plans for the protected site. Thanked the board for attention to the new 
stairway down to the public beach.

addressed by Town Attorney Howard.

presence of Immigration enforcement efforts.  
Laurie Macinello- OLE advocate, feels the town should address Immigration 
enforcement efforts.  
Desmond Wong- OLE advocate, via ZOOM, asked the supervisor to write a 
response to his several inquiries on procedures and asked to have them posted on 
the agenda.

the expansion of the Ambulance Barn located on Osborne Ave,  
ZOOM — Kerry Flanagan — alluded to a social media post picked up on social 
media platforms to a response posted by Council Member Waski, and the fear and 
apprehension felt by the Latino community in the wake of ICE killing innocent 
people. Questioned if Council Member Waski's comments were in support of a 
safety law to protect the community as a whole. 
ZOOM — Kevin Shea — Baiting Hollow — Thanked board and Council Member 
Waski for her frankness and publicly stating her positions. Encouraged her to 
reconsider and look at the OLE legislation.  
ZOOM — Kathy McGraw Advocated for OLE, and criticized the action of 
Immigration Enforcement efforts, illustrating a few incidents.  
ZOOM — Ron Harari supports Law Enforcement, but feels the town should be 
critical of ICE and commit to dialogue with OLE. 
John McAuliff — More discussion on OLE and towns responsibility 
Colin Tooker — Announced there will be a vigil held July 29th, to be for all the

for victims of all crimes who have lost their lives.  
ZOOM — Ron Harari — Wanted clarification on the Peconic Hotel and when the
