The Town Of Riverhead Hazardous Materials Response Team 
and Hazardous Materials Emergency Response  
Plan And Resource Guide 
 
 
Section 1. Establishment 
 
The Town Board hereby establishes the Town of Riverhead Hazardous Materials  
Response Team as an official municipal emergency response organization operating 
under the Riverhead Town Fire Marshal's Office. 
 
Section 2. Purpose 
 
The Hazardous Materials Response Team is established for the purpose of providing 
specialized emergency response to hazardous materials incidents occurring within the 
Town of Riverhead and providing mutual aid assistance to neighboring jurisdictions 
when authorized. 
 
Section 3. Administration 
 
The Riverhead Town Fire Marshal's Office shall administer the Hazardous Materials 
Response Team and shall be responsible for: 
Team organization and management; 
Personnel selection and qualification; 
Training and certification; 
Equipment acquisition and maintenance; 
Operational readiness; 
Budget administration; 
Recordkeeping; 
Policy implementation; 
Mutual aid coordination; and 
Compliance with all applicable laws and standards. 
 
Section 4. Adoption of Plan 
 
The Town Board hereby adopts the Town of Riverhead Hazardous Materials 
Emergency Response Plan and Resource Guide, together with future revisions 
approved by the Chief Fire Marshal and Town Board when required by law or Town policy, 
as the official operational manual governing hazardous materials emergency response 
within the Town of Riverhead. 
 
Section 5. Authority 
 
The Chief Fire Marshal, Assistant Chief Fire Marshal, and other designated supervisory 
personnel are authorized to implement the provisions of the Hazardous Materials 
Page 81 of 237
Emergency Response Plan and Resource Guide and to issue operational procedures 
consistent with the adopted Plan. 
 
Section 6. Mutual Aid 
 
The Hazardous Materials Response Team is authorized to participate in mutual aid

D&B Engineers and Architects (D&B) is pleased to provide this proposal to assist the Town of 
Riverhead (Town) in preparing a Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP) in accordance 
with the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation (DEC) requirements for the 
construction activities related to the Riverhead Town Square project.   
 
Construction activities involving soil disturbances of one (1) or more acres with the potential to 
discharge stormwater to surface waters of the State require coverage under the DEC State Pollutant 
Discharge Elimination System (SPDES) General Permit for St ormwater Discharges from 
Construction Activity ( CGP). Based on our review of the plans provided, the Riverhead Town 
Square project will have a disturbed area exceeding one (1) acre.   
 
D&B will review the site plans, erosion control plan, grading and drainage plan, and associated 
specifications provided by the Town for conformance with the requirements of CGP, New York 
State Standards and Specifications for Erosion and Sediment Control – November 2016 (Blue 
Book), and New York State Stormwater Management Design Manual – July 2024. D&B will 
provide comments and/or requests for additional information required to prepare a comprehensive 
SWPPP.  
 
Erosion and Sediment Control Plan 
 
Based on the construction details and schedule to be provided by the Town, D&B will prepare an 
Erosion and Sediment Control Plan describing the controls designed to minimize soil erosion and 
the discharge of sediment from the site. The plan will include a temporary and permanent soil 
stabilization plan and schedule. A narrative explanation of the measures proposed for minimizing 
soil compaction, preserving topsoil and completing soil restoration will be provided. D&B will

Chapter 207. Animals 
Article IV. Prohibition on Sales of Commercially Bred Dogs, Cats and Rabbits in Pet Stores 
§ 207-26. Purpose. 
It is the purpose and intent of the Town of Riverhead to promote animal welfare and encourage 
best practices in the breeding, sale, care and purchasing of dogs, cats and rabbits offered for retail 
sale in the Town of Riverhead. This Article will harmonize the Town Code with the recent NYS 
Puppy Mill Pipeline Law (PMPL) by eliminating such provisions of Chapter 207, Article IV 
which have been or may be superseded by the PMPL, and, in addition, modernize and update 
animal welfare standards concerning persons and entities engaged in the retail sale of 
companion animals in order to ensure the health, safety and welfare of those companion 
animals.

§ 207-32. Animal Maintenance Requirements  
This section shall refer only to dogs, cats and rabbits maintained or displayed for sale within a 
facility located within the Town of Riverhead only and the following shall be mandatory:  
1.  Each dog, cat or rabbit shall have an appropriate primary enclosure.  
2.  An operator shall ensure that a primary enclosure is constructed and maintained in 
such a manner as to protect the dogs, cats or rabbits inside from injury, prevent the 
dogs, cats and rabbits inside from escaping, keep other animals out, and allow the 
dogs, cats or rabbits inside convenient access to food and water. In addition, an 
operator shall enable the dogs, cats or rabbits inside to remain clean and dry and 
free of vermin as required by species and provide sufficient space for each dog, cat 
or rabbit inside to turn about freely and to stand, sit and lie in a comfortable, normal 
position as appropriate for the species. The minimum area per dog, cat and rabbit 
animal in each enclosure shall be no less than 20 sq. ft per animal.

3.  Dogs, cats and rabbits of the same species who are observed to coexist amicably 
with each other shall be permitted to be enclosed together.  Dogs, cats and rabbits 
who have been observed to fight or display aggressive or incompatible behavior 
toward each other shall not be placed in a separate enclosure. No species shall be 
exhibited I the same cage or enclosure as another species. 
4.  Dog, cat and rabbit enclosures shall not be sprayed with insecticide while an animal 
is enclosed or within 24 hours prior to an animal being enclosed in a sprayed area.   
5.  With respect to primary enclosures for dogs, a pet shop, pet store, pet shop outlet

a.  Not displayed in a manner that allows unrestricted access by the public; and

injury or trap a limb or other body part and which is designed to minimize 
stress and cross‐contamination.

at all times be maintained at temperatures between 68 Fahrenheit and 77 Fahrenheit 
unless otherwise specified by NYS law, or NYS Department of Agriculture and

TB Resolution 2026-812 
 
AUTHORIZES DESIGNATED ALCOHOL SERVICE VENDOR TO SERVE ALCOHOL AT THE BLUES BY 
THE BAY EVENT IN GRANGEBEL PARK 
 
Councilwoman Waski offered the following resolution,  
which was seconded by Councilman Rothwell 
 
          WHEREAS, the Riverhead Town Board approved a Chapter 255 special events permit by 
Resolution No. 2026-    adopted on August 18, 2026 to conduct “Blues by the River”, on 
Saturday, August 29, 2026 from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., at Grangebel Park, Riverhead, New 
York; and 
 
          WHEREAS, Cutchogue Lion’s Club has requested permission to allow alcohol service 
from designated vendors within the licensed premises at the subject place, date and time; and 
 
          WHEREAS, Cutchogue Lion’s Club has designated the following alcohol service vendor 
for participation regarding the subject event:  
 
          1. SC Net Wirth Inc., d/b/a Digger’s Fine Food & Spirits  
 
          NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Town of Riverhead approves the 
above-referenced alcohol service vendor regarding alcohol service at “Blues by the River” on 
Saturday, August 29, 2026 from 10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., at Grangebel Park, Riverhead, New 
York, subject to the following conditions: 
 
a.   The participating alcohol service vendor shall comply with New York State Alcohol 
Beverage Control Laws, NYS Liquor Authority Rules and Regulations and the Town of 
Riverhead Alcohol Policy, as applicable. 
 
b.    The participating alcohol service vendor shall execute a license agreement in a form 
to be approved by the Town Attorney’s Office. 
 
c.    The participating alcohol service vendor shall procure a general liability insurance 
policy for the subject activity naming the Town of Riverhead and Riverhead Townscape 
Incorporated, as “additional insureds” on a primary and non-contributory basis, with 
policy limits of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence/ $2,000,000 aggregate, including 
food/product service liability protection, if applicable.  In addition to the above referenced 
insurance requirements, alcohol vendors shall also procure an additional insurance 
liability policy regarding alcohol service liability with policy limits of at least 
$2,000,000.00 dollars per occurrence/$2,000,000.00 dollars aggregate which shall name 
the above-referenced parties as additional insureds also on a primary and non-
contributory basis. 
 
 d.     Alcohol service shall also be subject to the following conditions: 
 
          A.    All alcohol servers must be TIPS (Training for Intervention Procedures) 
Page 198 of 237
approved by New York State. 
          B.    Alcohol service and consumption shall be limited to the authorized and 
approved alcohol service vendors named herein as limited to the licensed premises (as 
delineated on the Riverhead Country Fair map), as follows. 
          C.    Alcohol service shall be solely limited to beer, cider, wine and/or champagne.   
          D.    Alcohol service shall be limited to two (2) drinks per age-appropriate -customer 
at a time. 
          E.    Licensee shall possess and provide, prior to participation, a valid New York 
State Liquor Authority catering permit, or other appropriate permit, regarding off-
premises alcohol service and comply with all applicable permit requirements including 
all other applicable municipal laws, rules and regulations, including service hours. 
          F.    Alcohol service shall be served in clear plastic cups and contain an identifying 
symbol and/or logo uniquely identifying the vendor. Each such cup shall be limited in 
size to 12 oz. for beer/cider; 5 oz. for wine/champagne. 
          G.    Alcohol servers must personally affix wristbands to the wrists of age-
appropriate customers in a manner that prevents easy removal and transferability, no 
exceptions.   
          H.    Such other conditions more fully delineated in the alcohol vendor license 
agreement and Town alcohol policy; and, be it further, 
 
          RESOLVED, that the Riverhead Town Code section 205-3, which prohibits the 
consumption of any alcoholic beverages in any public place within the Town of Riverhead and 
also prohibits the possession of any open or unsealed container of an alcoholic beverage while 
in a public place for the purposes of consumption, is hereby suspended for enforcement 
purposes solely regarding the “Blues by the River” on Saturday, August 29, 2026 from 10:00 
a.m. to 5:00 p.m., subject to the above-stated requirements, solely limited to the licensed 
premises as delineated on the “Blues by the River map” and subject to the New York State 
Alcohol Beverage Control Law and NYS Liquor Authority Rules and Regulations; and it be 
further,   
 
          RESOLVED, that the Town of Riverhead waives the Town of Riverhead alcohol policy 
requirement regarding submission of a security/damage deposit by participating alcohol service 
vendors; and be it further, 
 
          RESOLVED, that the Town Supervisor is authorized to execute license agreements 
regarding alcohol service vendors in a form to be approved by the Town Attorney’s Office; and 
be it further, 
 
          RESOLVED, that the Town Clerk is hereby directed to forward a Certified copy of this 
resolution to the Riverhead Town Police Department, Office of the Fire Marshal and the Town 
Attorney’s Office; and be it further,  
 
          RESOLVED, that all Town Hall Departments may review and obtain a copy of this 
resolution from the electronic storage device and, if needed, a certified copy of same may be 
obtained from the Office of the Town Clerk.
