NOTICE OF PUBLIC HEARING 
TOWN OF RIVERHEAD AND TOWN OF RIVERHEAD COMMUNITY 
DEVELOPMENT AGENCY TOWN OF RIVERHEAD, SUFFOLK COUNTY, NEW YORK 
 
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN, that the Town of Riverhead, by its Town Board as legislative 
body of the Town of Riverhead and governing body of the Community Development 
Agency, Town of Riverhead, Suffolk County, New York will hold a public hearing at 
Riverhead Town Hall, 4 West Second Street,  Riverhead, New York 11901, on October 
16, 2025 at 4:00 pm, prevailing time, pursuant to General Municipal Law 507(2)(d) for the 
purpose of considering whether The Jazz Loft Inc., a not-for-profit corporation, having its 
principal address at 275 Christian Avenue, Stony Brook, New York 11790 should be 
designated the "qualified and eligible sponsor" for the purchase and redevelopment of 
property described as SCTM# 0600-128.00-6.00-080.00 located at 18 Peconic Avenue, 
Riverhead, New York 11901 pursuant to a certain proposed Contract of Sale outlining the 
sale and redevelopment of the property, which said Contract of Sale is on file in the office 
of the Town Clerk of the Town of Riverhead and is available for public inspection during 
regular business hours. Pursuant to the Contract of Sale, The Jazz Loft Inc. will redevelop 
the property it acquires including the renovation and operation of the subject premises to 
be used only for educational, recreational and/or any other cultural purposes and shall 
maintain its historical character. 
At said public hearing, the Town of Riverhead, by its Town Board as legislative 
body of the Town of Riverhead and governing body of the Community Development 
Agency, will hear all persons interested in the subject matter thereof.
