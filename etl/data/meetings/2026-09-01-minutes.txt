 ● 2:05 PM Public Hearing - SCWA Monroe Balancing Test  
Opened at 2:42. TA Howard read the public notice for the meeting. Planner Matt 
Charters gave an overview of the project consisting of water lines being erected 
throughout Riverhead to serve the east end of Southold Town and the effect it may 
have on the local residents and infrastructure. Jeffrey Seaman environmental 
consultant for the town in this matter. Gave a lengthy history of lack of 
transparency with the SCWA in regard to SEQRA.  
Public-Mike Foley commended Jeff Seaman for his presentation. Criticized Jeff 
Zabo from SCWA for being misleading.  
Warren McKnight — Spoke about commercial visitors to Riverhead. 
Claudette Bianco — opposed to SCWA expansion. 
The attorney for SCWA passed up 2 binders of response for the Monroe Hearing 
consisting of their Draft EIS  
John McAuliff — opposition to SCWA extension, states that there is no record of 
Southold residents wanting additional water.  
Barbara Blass- Spoke about the Monroe test, and stated attending the county's 
hearing, Stated being upset that this is dumping on Riverhead.  
Stan Carey — questioned the town's position and task taken as out of character 
and stated never done before.  
Closed 4:48, held open for ten days for written comment
