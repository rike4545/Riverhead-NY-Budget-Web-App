LEGAL SERVICES CONTRACT RELATED TO AND IN FURTHANCE OF TOWN LAW 
64-e  INTERMUNCIPAL AGREEMENT 
 
 
THIS AGREEMENT, made this   day of November, 2026 between the TOWN OF 
RIVERHEAD, with its principal offices at 4 West Second Street, Riverhead, New York 
11901 ("Town"), and Mary Wilson, Esq., 933 Mill Creek Drive, Palm Beach Gardens, 
Florida 33410 (Legal Counsel).  
 
WHEREAS, pursuant to the requirements of New York State Town Law 64-e 
"Peconic Bay region preservation funds" the five Town East End Towns of East Hampton, 
Riverhead, Shelter Island, Southampton, and Southold (collectively the "Member 
Towns"), all of which have established community preservation funds, created, by way of 
an Intermunicipal Agreement (the "IMA"), the Peconic Bay Region Community 
Preservation Fund Advisory Committee (the "Advisory Committee") to render legal 
opinions and interpretations to facilitate the efficient and consistent administration of each 
fund; and 
 
WHEREAS, in further compliance with the requirements of New York State Town 
Law 64-e, the Towns established the office of Regional Counsel for the Advisory 
Committee who shall render interpretations and/or opinions to the Advisory Committee 
upon request by a majority of the Member Towns, and 
 
WHEREAS, Mary C. Wilson, Esq. submitted a proposal to serve as Counsel for 
the Regional Community Preservation Fund Advisory Committee, and the Member Towns 
are in  agreement to retain Ms. Wilson as Counsel;  
 
NOW, THEREFORE, it is agreed as follows:  
 
I. Legal Counsel shall serve as Regional Counsel to the Advisory Committee and 
shall provide legal services to such Advisory Committee in a manner consistent with the 
IMA signed by the Member Towns, a copy of which is attached hereto and deemed a part 
hereof. Legal Counsel shall serve in the position of Regional Counsel at the pleasure of 
the Member Towns and may be subject to removal by Member Towns as provided for in 
the IMA.  
 
2. Consistent with the terms of the IMA, this Contract shall be effective for the 2026 
calendar year. This Agreement shall be subject to extension or renewal consistent with 
the provisions and terms of the IMA.  
 
3. Legal counsel shall be paid for legal services to the Advisory Committee set 
forth in her proposal of January 10, 2025, a copy of which is attached hereto, which 
provides for payment at a rate of $400.00 per hour for legal services together with 
reimbursement for additional expenses for travel time, telecopies, photocopying and 
printing fees, as the same may be incurred from time to time. Legal counsel shall provide 
periodic invoices, quarterly, bi-annually or annually at the discretion of regional counsel, 
together with such other documentation as may be required by the Member Towns to 
facilitate reimbursement for expenses, and Member Towns reserve the right to require 
further documentation or information, if necessary, to process payments. Payments shall 
be paid from the appropriate capital budget account. Legal Counsel shall not be required 
to prepare individual invoices for each Member Town, but shall provide a copy of the 
complete invoice to each such Member Town. Member Towns shall be responsible for 
allocating the payment to Legal Counsel based upon the agreed upon allocation set forth 
in the IMA. No Member Town shall be responsible for payment of legal costs above their 
respective share as provided in the IMA.  
 
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have entered into this Agreement the date 
first written above.
