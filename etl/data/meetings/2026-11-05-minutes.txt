VII.  Public Hearings 
 ● 2:00 PM Public Hearing - Lateral Water Main Extension - 1001 Scott Avenue, 
Calverton  
 The hearing was opened at 2:07pm , the Water Superintendent, Frank Mancini, 
gave an overview of the extension. The hearing was held open till Nov 15th for 
written comment. All costs will be paid by the developer. 
 ● 2:00 PM Public Hearing - Art.279-7B - Taxation Exemption  
The hearing opened at 2:11 pm, Town Attorney Erik Howard gave an overview of 
the exception for disabled veterans. Rothwell clarified the history of the State 
legislation. Lavern Tennenberg addressed the board as to wording and sections 
that may need to be clarified.  — Paul Lescenski spoke in favor of the legislation to 
assist our veterans in this manner. —Commander of the American Legion of 
Riverhead also spoke to support the legislation.  The hearing was held open for 10 
days for written comment till Nov 15th. 
 ● 2:05 PM Public Hearing - To Consider Adoption of 2027 Budget  
The hearing was called to order at 2:32 pm. Jeanette Dipaola gave an overview of 
the budget. No public comment. Held open for written comment for 10 days. Nov 
15th.2026
