Budget accuracy

A budget is a promise about what things will cost. These are the lines where that promise and the actual spending drift far enough apart to be worth asking about — 11 researched by hand, plus 116 found automatically in the 2027 Budget Supplement and more across 8 years of Supplements.

Independent project·Updated September 25, 2026·● Current·Check data freshness →
💡In plain English
Of the 11 researched lines below, 4 ran over budget in both 2024 and 2025 and are budgeted below what they cost again in the 2027 Tentative. Across every fund, 3 lines were over budget in each of the last three years, and 22 have been budgeted year after year with nothing spent. Separately, the 2027 Supplement budgets $2,026,270 above the trailing run-rate on controllable lines.
  • ›Why it matters: a line budgeted at a fraction of what it really costs makes the whole budget look smaller than it is, and the gap has to be absorbed somewhere later in the year.
  • ›Not the same as overspending: a line can be over budget because a bill genuinely rose, or because the budgeted figure was never realistic. The point is to ask which.
  • ›What is excluded: mandated costs — pension, workers’ compensation, insurance, debt service, payroll taxes — and revenue lines. Their variance is obligation or timing, not discretion.

Uniform OT means overtime for sworn uniformed police personnel. It is driven by minimum staffing rules, vacancies, sick or vacation backfill, shift coverage, arrests, court time, events, emergencies, training coverage, and contract overtime premiums.

Over budget in every year shown: $1,072,181 against $700,000 in 2023, $1,401,354 against $700,011 in 2024, $1,113,609 against $1,000,000 in 2025. The budget was cut from $1,100,000 in 2022 to $700,000 for 2023, less than the line had cost in any year since 2018. The 2027 Tentative's $1,037,500 is below what it cost in each of the last three years. March workload data complicates the offset story: criminal incidents rose to 167 from 144 and total incidents rose to 2,994 from 2,922, even though accidents and summonses fell.

Ask: Either budget overtime at what it has cost, or publish a monthly overtime report showing how the gap between the $1,037,500 budget and the $1,072,181–$1,401,354 the line has cost will be closed through scheduling, cause coding, and tighter court, recall, training and event review, without assuming workload has declined.
