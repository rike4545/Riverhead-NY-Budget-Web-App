Legal costs

What the Town spends on lawyers, inside and outside the Town Attorney's office, and what it pays on claims against it, 2018 to the 2027 Preliminary budget.

Independent, unofficial project·Data last refreshed November 8, 2026·Recently refreshed·See data update details →

In plain English

The Town Attorney's line for outside lawyers was budgeted at $400,000 for 2026 and has cost more than its budget in six of the seven years since 2019. In 2025 it cost $818,961 against a $400,000 budget. By June 30, 2026 it had already spent $405,990, more than the whole year's budget, and the 2027 Preliminary budgets $400,000 again.
  • Where the figures come from: the Town’s own Budget Supplements, which print each account’s actual spending for past years, its adopted budget, and what it had spent by June 30 of the current year.
  • What is counted: the Town Attorney’s office and every account the Supplements label as legal professional services. Claims paid by the Town’s self-insurance fund are shown on their own, because they pay claimants, not lawyers.
  • What is not here: which firm was paid for which case. The Supplements show one line for all outside counsel; the invoices behind it are in the Town’s claims records.

Outside lawyers: budget against cost

The Town Attorney's office hires law firms and special counsel for cases and work its own staff does not take on, such as tax certiorari cases, appeals and litigation with other governments. All of it is paid from one account, A01-1-1420-433-000-00000. The budget has stayed at $400,000 since 2022 while the cost has not.

One matter's cost has been reported separately: the Town's share of the even-year election lawsuit, $207,958.79 through December 31, 2025, as reported by RiverheadLOCAL. The Supplement does not say which account paid it. Read about the election law case.

All legal services, year by year

The four groups together cost $11,133,193 from 2018 through 2025. Staff pay is the Town Attorney's own lawyers and staff; outside lawyers are the firms it hires and those other departments hire.

Claims against the Town

The Town's Risk Retention fund makes what the Supplement calls “Self Insurance Payments” (MS2-1-1930-487-000-00000), under function 1930, judgments and claims, in the State's chart of accounts. It paid $533,867 in 2025 against a $450,000 budget, and $318,266 of its $450,000 2026 budget by June 30.

On November 5, 2026, the Board moved $225,000 to cover “additional general liability insurance claims incurred during the current fiscal year” (resolution 2026-970). Each operating fund also has a “Gen Liab - Claim Payments” line. Those are not added here: they may be what the funds pay into the same self-insurance arrangement, and adding both could count the same dollar twice.

Questions residents can ask

  • Outside counsel has cost more than $400,000 in most years. Why does the 2027 Preliminary budget it at $400,000 again, and which account pays when it runs over?
  • Which firms were paid from the outside-counsel line in 2025 and 2026, for which matters, and how much each?
  • What did each settlement cost, and was it paid from the self-insurance fund, an insurer or the General Fund?

Invoices the Town has paid are public records. Request records under the Freedom of Information Law.
