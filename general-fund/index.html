General Fund History, 2005–2026

How the Town's principal operating fund has grown from 2005 to 2026: appropriations, tax levy, estimated revenues, and reserve use, straight from the adopted budgets.

Independent project·Updated October 10, 2026·Current·Check data freshness →

In plain English

This page shows the General Fund from 2005 to 2026 — the main town budget — so you can see how spending and the property-tax bill have changed over time.
  • Appropriations: the dark line = total planned spending. Tax levy (gold) = the amount raised from property taxes. Revenues (green) = other income like fees and state aid.
  • Why it matters: when the tax-levy line rises faster than spending, more of the budget is being paid for by property taxes.
  • Adopted figures: these are the budgeted plans approved each year, not the final year-end actuals.
The Town’s 2027 Tentative Budget is out. It proposes a town-wide tax levy of $62,882,202, up 2.79% from 2026, for the General Fund, Highway and Street Lighting. With the special districts, the levy is $67,159,863, up 2.78% from 2026 and $1,807,211 less than this site’s forecast of $68,967,074. The Supervisor’s letter says every taxing district is within the tax cap limit of 2.79%, and no district’s levy rises by more than that. For the General Fund it proposes $72,014,362 of spending, up $2,901,203 or 4.20% from 2026’s $69,113,159, and a levy of $54,336,950, up 2.8% from 2026, using $950,000 of reserves.
