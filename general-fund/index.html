General Fund History, 2005–2027

How the Town's principal operating fund has grown from 2005 to 2027: appropriations, tax levy, estimated revenues, and reserve use, straight from the adopted budgets.

Independent, unofficial project·Data last refreshed November 25, 2026·Recently refreshed·See data update details →

In plain English

This page shows the General Fund from 2005 to 2027 — the main town budget — so you can see how spending and the property-tax bill have changed over time.
  • Appropriations: the dark line = total planned spending. Tax levy (gold) = the amount raised from property taxes. Revenues (green) = other income like fees and state aid.
  • Why it matters: when the tax-levy line rises faster than spending, more of the budget is being paid for by property taxes.
  • Adopted figures: these are the budgeted plans approved each year, not the final year-end actuals.
The 2027 budget is adopted.

The Town Board adopted it on November 17, 2026 (Resolution 2026-995, 5–0), unchanged from the Preliminary: $120,854,219 across all funds. The town-wide levy is $62,238,344, up 1.73% from 2026, and the town-wide tax rate is $72.487 per $1,000 of assessed value, against $71.598 in 2026.
