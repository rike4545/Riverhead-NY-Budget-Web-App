Recreation Program Fund (A06)

Recreation programs meant to be paid for by program fees rather than property taxes. The audits have found it in deficit at every year-end since 2023.

The audit and the Annual Financial Report both report actuals for the combined General Fund group, which includes this fund and its sibling funds — so these figures cover the whole group, not this budget fund alone.

Audited, from the 2025 financial statements the Town Board accepted on September 1, 2026; money in and out include transfers between funds. The Town's own unaudited Annual Financial Report has $71,010,826 in, $66,007,499 out, a $5,003,327 surplus and $33,407,251 at year-end.

In deficit at the end of every audited year: $692,688 at the end of 2025

The Town's auditors report this fund inside the General Fund, and each audit since 2023 has found it in deficit at year-end. Each has also said the deficit would be gone the following year “by reducing the expenditures and increasing the program revenues.” Each year it grew instead. No budget has planned for it to close: every one since 2024 sets this fund's revenues exactly equal to its spending, which at best keeps the deficit from growing. Closing it would take a year that brings in $692,688 more than it spends; the 2027 Tentative does not plan one either. Because the fund is reported inside the General Fund, the deficit is already netted out of that fund's $33,303,051 balance.
