💡In plain English
This page shows where the Town plans to spend money in 2026, organized into separate pots called funds. The biggest is the General Fund, which pays for most town-wide services.
  • ›A "fund": is a separate pot of money for a purpose — General (most services), Highway, Water, Sewer, and so on. Each has its own balanced budget.
  • ›Click any fund: to open it and see the departments and individual spending lines inside it, with multi-year trends.
  • ›"Reconciled": means our line-item totals add up exactly to the Town’s official published numbers.
