Budget flow

One picture of the 2027 Preliminary budget: every dollar from its kind of revenue, through the Town's funds, to what it pays for.

Independent, unofficial project·Data last refreshed November 10, 2026·Recently refreshed·See data update details →

In plain English

The 2027 Preliminary budget is $120,854,219 across all funds. Property taxes bring in $71,818,995, 59.4% of it. Some money is counted twice because it moves between funds: $5,834,128 in transfers and up to $2,037,700 in charges one fund pays another, which the State groups with other local income. Both appear as money in and money out.
  • Left: where the money comes from, grouped by the State’s revenue codes.
  • Middle: the funds it goes into. Each fund is its own account; the General Fund is the largest.
  • Right: what it pays for, grouped by the State’s function codes.

Sources and method

  • Every line of the 2027 Preliminary Budget (PDF), as the site reads it for the 2027 budget pages.
  • Revenue is grouped by its account's source code and spending by its function code, following the State Comptroller's chart of accounts for towns. “Employee benefits” are the benefits budgeted centrally (codes 90 and 91); salaries are in each function.
  • PILOTs are payments in lieu of taxes: what some businesses pay instead of property tax under agreements with the Town or its Industrial Development Agency.
  • “Savings” is appropriated fund balance: money from past years the budget plans to spend. See savings used.
