Resolution builder

For Town staff drafting a Town Board resolution: write what each clause says, and the builder lays it out the way the Town’s agenda packets print it, with the joining words, capitals, budget table and closing clauses in place.

Independent, unofficial project·Data last refreshed November 11, 2026·Recently refreshed·See data update details →

In plain English

Resolutions in the 2026 agenda packets follow one layout: the number and a title in capitals, who offered and seconded it, WHEREAS clauses joined by “; and”, then “Now, therefore, be it” and RESOLVED clauses joined by “; and be it further”, with any budget adjustment as a FROM / TO table and the same two closing clauses. This builder writes that structure so you only write the substance. It is an aid, not the Town’s template: the Town Attorney and the Accounting Department review every resolution, and the Town Clerk assigns its number. Every resolution also needs a Fiscal Impact Statement; the Fiscal impact statement helper fills one in from the same accounts.
  • Starting points: a donation, a budget transfer, an appointment and an agreement, each following an adopted 2026 resolution’s wording with blanks in square brackets.
  • Checks: the builder flags blanks left in brackets, a budget table that does not balance, an amount in the clauses that differs from the table, and fund balance that the Fiscal Impact Statement must not mark absorbed.
  • Private: everything runs in your browser. The draft is kept in this browser only, and nothing is sent anywhere.

3. Why: WHEREAS clauses

One reason per box. The builder adds “WHEREAS,” at the start, “; and” between clauses, and “Now, therefore, be it” after the last.

4. What the Board decides: RESOLVED clauses

One decision per box. The builder adds “RESOLVED, that” and “; and be it further”. The budget table, the copies and the Town Clerk clause are added after these.

5. Budget adjustment (if money moves)

Type or pick an account code; the name fills in from the 2027 Budget Supplement. Put the source of the money under FROM and where it goes under TO.

6. Who gets a copy

Departments or offices the Town Clerk sends the resolution to, such as the Financial Administrator, payroll or the Office of the Town Attorney.

The closing clause every resolution ends with, that Town Hall departments may get a copy from the Town Clerk, is always added.

7. Checks

3 items need attention before this is ready.

  • Choose who offers the resolution and who seconds it.
  • Fill in the blanks still in square brackets: [Donation], [Department], [a donation / donations], [amount] and more.
  • A01-2705-000-00000-J has no amount.
  • The budget table balances at $0.00.
