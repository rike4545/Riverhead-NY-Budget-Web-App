Donation map

Where each Town Board candidate's and party committee's itemized contributions came from, by ZIP code.

Independent, unofficial project·Data last refreshed November 8, 2026·Recently refreshed·See data update details →

In plain English

Of the 11 committees, Jerome Halpin raised the largest share of its itemized money from ZIP codes that reach into Riverhead, 68%, and Laura Jens-Smith the smallest, 20%, all years together. Choose a committee and a year below.
  • ZIP codes, not names: the map adds up money by the ZIP code on each filing. It does not show donors’ names or street addresses, though the filings themselves are public.
  • Itemized contributions: money from people, businesses and others that the committee itemized on its filings (Schedules A, B and C). Transfers from other committees, in-kind gifts and loans are left out, and so are small contributions a committee did not itemize.
  • ZIP codes cross town lines: Wading River (11792), Calverton (11933) and Manorville (11949) reach into Brookhaven, and Riverhead’s own 11901 takes in Flanders and Riverside across the river in Southampton, so “reach into Riverhead” includes some money from outside the Town.

Sources and method

  • Contributions: New York State Board of Elections, Campaign Finance Disclosure Reports Data (data.ny.gov), 2005 to 2026, for the committees on the Campaign finance page. Each committee's total here equals the “direct contributions” on its card there. Updated November 7, 2026; refreshed with the campaign-finance data by etl/build_donation_map.py.
  • ZIP codes are as filed. A ZIP code entered wrongly on a filing stays as entered, and its place name is the one most often written beside it. Money with no ZIP code is counted on its own line.
  • ZIP code areas: U.S. Census Bureau, 2020 ZIP Code Tabulation Areas (cartographic boundaries, 1:500,000). The Town's outline is the County Board of Elections' district map, as on the Election map. The map shows the ZIP codes around Riverhead; money from farther away is in the breakdown and the table. Bands are $1,000, $5,000, $25,000, $100,000.
