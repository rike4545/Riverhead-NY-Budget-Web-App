How to read the source hierarchy

1 · Riverhead record
Adopted budgets, AFRs, audits, minutes, payroll files and other Town records establish what Riverhead reported, adopted, paid or voted on.
2 · GASB / OSC authority
Governmental GAAP and State Comptroller guidance establish the accounting, tax-cap, budget, procurement and reporting framework used to interpret those records.
3 · Site calculation
Comparisons, projections and scenario outputs are calculations by this site. They should be reproducible from cited records and a stated method.

Related accounting references · different scope

Useful, but not Riverhead’s governing municipal GAAP

These references can matter when a Town transaction involves a nongovernmental counterparty. They remain separate so the site does not imply that private-sector GAAP governs Riverhead’s own financial statements.

FASB Accounting Standards Codification (ASC)

Financial Accounting Standards Board
Checked Sep 7, 2026Availability monitored

Scope: Authoritative U.S. GAAP recognized by FASB for nongovernmental entities.

Useful when a Riverhead transaction also involves a private company, nonprofit or other nongovernmental counterparty. It is not the primary accounting authority for the Town of Riverhead itself: state and local governmental financial reporting follows GASB authoritative GAAP, with New York local-government implementation and reporting guidance supplied by OSC.

External authority monitoring

The pull-request Quality Gate now requests every authority URL. A missing or unreachable source fails the check. Stable OSC publication files are SHA-256 fingerprinted; after a reviewed baseline is stored, a changed fingerprint is surfaced for human review rather than silently treated as the same authority.

Dynamic portals such as the OSC tax-cap landing page and FASB ASC use availability monitoring instead of full-page hashing because routine HTML changes do not necessarily mean the underlying accounting guidance changed.

What updates itself, behind the scenes

Searchable Financial RecordsactiveOne unified search across budget line items, employee payroll, Board-authorized salaries, Town Board votes, operating funds, and 12,000+ parsed document pages, with live filtering by record type.
Town Board Voting RecordsactiveEvery recorded Town Board vote since January 2025 — 39 meetings and 1,672 resolutions with results, movers, seconders, and how each member voted — plus per-member career records listing every dissent and abstention.
Payroll and Overtime IntelligenceactiveActual pay for every employee 2018–2025 (base, overtime summed from detailed pay codes, gross), Board-authorized salaries for 2025 and 2026, and a raise-by-raise comparison with promotions flagged. The 2025 authorized figures now compare against same-year 2025 actual pay.
Cross-Document Financial ReconciliationactiveExtracted budget line items reconcile to the dollar against the official Summary page for all 19 funds; 2025 actual results (AFR) are compared against the adopted plan town-wide and on every fund page; 2024 audited fund balances are cross-checked.
Multi-Year Fiscal Trend AnalysisactiveBudget Compare tracks every fund’s appropriations 2020–2026; the General Fund page charts two decades (2005–2025); each of 848 account line items carries its own seven-year trend; payroll trends span 2018–2025.
Department and Operational ComparisonsactiveEvery fund drills down to departments with spending-category rollups (salaries, contractual, equipment, benefits) and year-over-year change; payroll is filterable by department and title from 2022 on.
Automated Fiscal IndicatorsactiveResident-facing indicators track the actual 2025 General Fund surplus, levy and appropriation growth, payroll and overtime totals, reserve use, and fund-balance reliance.
Reserve and Fund Balance MonitoringactiveTracks appropriated fund-balance use in the budget and the actual year-end picture: the General Fund’s savings grew to $33.4M in 2025, broken down by how freely each classification can be spent.
Tax Levy and Stabilization ViewsactiveLevy history for the General Fund back to 2005, current-year levy dependency for every fund, and a reserve-supported stabilization scenario with stated assumptions.
Debt and Capital Financing AnalyticspartialDebt Service Fund appropriations and actual debt-service spending are on the fund pages and Annual Report; dedicated schedules (principal/interest, BAN exposure) are not yet extracted.
Plain-English ExplanationsactiveEvery tool opens with an “In plain English” intro; a Start Here guide, a full budget glossary, tappable term definitions, and column guides explain the record to readers with no finance background.
Automated Data PipelineactiveA weekly run fetches new meeting minutes from the Town’s CivicClerk portal and re-scrapes the Town’s Financial Reports page for new PDFs, parses everything with a dedicated parser per document type (budget, AFR, payroll, salary schedule, meeting votes), rebuilds search and CSV downloads, verifies the build, commits changes, and redeploys the site.
Open Data DownloadsactiveTen datasets — payroll, salaries, raises, votes, member records, line items, fund history, and actual results — free to download as CSV for spreadsheets or JSON for developers, regenerated automatically.
