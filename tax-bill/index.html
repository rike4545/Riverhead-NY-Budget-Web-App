My tax bill — what would I actually pay?

Estimate the Town's portion of your property-tax bill at the 2025 and 2026 adopted rates and the proposed 2027 rate, from the Town's own budget books. This covers only the Town: county, school, fire and library taxes are billed separately.

The Town-wide tax rate, per $1,000 of assessed value

Rates from the Town's budget books. Reported directly in a Town or other cited official record.

The 2027 Preliminary Budget sets the rate at 72.487, up 0.889 (1.2%) from 2026. It is not final: the Town Board adopts the 2027 budget by November 20, and the rate is then reset on the final assessment roll, as 2026's moved from 71.549 to 71.598.

In plain English

Official published rate table. Reported directly in a Town or other cited official record.Enter the assessed value from your tax bill to see your Town taxes at each year's rate. The 2025 and 2026 rates are the adopted ones (Rate table, p. 6: 'Total Town Wide' rate, updated based on the final assessment roll.); the 2027 rate is the latest proposal.
  • Assessed value, not market value: Riverhead assesses property at a small fraction of market value, 7.73% on the 2026 roll. The median one-family home is assessed at about $46,300, which stands for a market value of about $598,965.
  • Town portion only: County, school, fire and library taxes are separate lines on your bill.
  • STAR: STAR lowers school taxes only. It does not change the Town portion, so this calculator does not ask for it.

Estimate your Town taxes

Get your own numbers first

Look up your property on Suffolk County Property Tax Services (opens the County’s site; it asks you to search by one field at a time). Copy your property’s assessed value, any Town exemptions and the bill’s total into the boxes below. Your paper bill or assessment notice has them too. This site never sees what you look up: the estimate is worked out in your browser and not saved.

Riverhead Budget Live is an independent project. It is not run, approved, or endorsed by the Town of Riverhead.
