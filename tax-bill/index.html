Rate change calculated from published rates

The Town-wide rate rose from 67.031 to 71.598 per $1,000.

That is an increase of 4.567 per $1,000, or about 6.8%. The calculator below translates that rate change into dollars for your property. Your actual total tax bill also includes county, school, fire, and library charges.
