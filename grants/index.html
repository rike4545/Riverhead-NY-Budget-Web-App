Grants

What the Town received in grants and State and federal aid from 2018 to 2025, and, for each grant the Town Board accepted since 2025, the award and the Town's share of the cost.

Independent, unofficial project·Data last refreshed November 8, 2026·Recently refreshed·See data update details →

In plain English

From 2018 to 2025 the Town received $26.83 million in grants and State and federal aid tied to a purpose: $15.20 million from the State and $11.63 million from the federal government. It rose from $1.65 million in 2018 to $7.42 million in 2025. That leaves out $13.29 million of mortgage tax and State revenue sharing, general aid every town receives.
  • Award: what the grant promises. Most grants pay the Town back after it spends the money, so an award can be received over several years.
  • Received: the grant money the Town recorded as revenue in a year, as it reported to the State Comptroller.
  • Cost share (match): the part of a project the Town or a partner must pay itself. Some grants require none; the page says so only where the resolution states it.

What it paid for

The Water District line is State aid the Water District recorded in 2023 and 2024, and in 2025 a $3,575,000 federal congressionally directed project for a water main extension (Resolution 2026-640). Roads and highways is the State's highway aid: CHIPS, PAVE NY, Pave Our Potholes and Extreme Winter Recovery, which the State pays every town by formula.

Federal awards spent in 2025

A town that spends enough federal money in a year must have a single audit, which lists every federal award it spent. Of the years since 2018, the Federal Audit Clearinghouse holds one for Riverhead only for 2025, with $6,616,741 spent. That matches, to the dollar, the federal aid the Town reported to the Comptroller for 2025. Most of it came through the State or the County rather than straight from Washington.

Sources and method

  • Office of the State Comptroller, Financial Data for Local Governments: every State aid (3000s) and federal aid (4000s) revenue account in the Town's Annual Financial Report, 2018 to 2025. Mortgage tax (3005) and State revenue sharing (3001) are left out of the grant totals because every town receives them.
  • Federal Audit Clearinghouse, single audit search: the Town's single audit (EIN 11-6001935). No other year since 2018 is on file there for the Town.
  • Town Board resolutions in the agenda packets on the Town's CivicClerk portal, quoted in full under “What each resolution says”. Applications the Board only authorized are not listed; only awards, allocations and agreements.
