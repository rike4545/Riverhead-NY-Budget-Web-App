Comprehensive Plan tracker

What the Town Board has and has not adopted from the Town of Riverhead 2024 Comprehensive Plan Update, recommendation by recommendation, as of November 7, 2026.

Independent, unofficial project·Data last refreshed November 8, 2026·Recently refreshed·See data update details →

In plain English

The Town Board adopted the plan on September 4, 2024 (Resolution 2024-760, unanimously). Of the 25 recommendations tracked here, 9 became law, 1 became law in part, and 3 saw a related law that does not say it carries out the plan. 2 are under way. 10 show no Town Board action in the records this site searched.
  • Comprehensive plan: the Town’s long-range guide for land use. It is not law by itself; zoning changes put it into effect.
  • Local law: a change to the Town Code, adopted by the Town Board after a public hearing.
  • No action found: this site found no Town Board resolution on the item. Work may still be going on in a department.

How it was put into effect

The plan replaced the 2003 Comprehensive Plan. It was prepared by BFJ Planning and runs to 210 pages. First tabled on August 21, 2024, it was adopted on September 4, 2024: minutes of that meeting.

Most of the work came at once. On December 17, 2024 the Board adopted 17 local laws tracked here, each of which says it carries out the plan: the new Calverton Industrial and Light Industrial districts, the rezonings, and the changes to the transfer of development rights (TDR) program. Since then, changes have come one at a time. See the TDR page for how credits work.

Adopted (13)

  • Law adoptedIndustrial areas

    Create a new Calverton Industrial (CI) district for the IND A land in Calverton, with larger setbacks and lower building coverage.

    “Create a new Calverton Industrial District (CI) for IND A”

    Plan, page 191

    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1016: Rezoned properties to the new Calverton Industrial (CI) district. The resolution says it carries out the plan.
    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1019: Removed the Industrial A (IND A) district. The resolution says it carries out the plan.
    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1024: Added the new districts to the list of zoning districts. The resolution says it carries out the plan.
    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1025: Added an industrial districts table of permitted uses for LI and CI. The resolution says it carries out the plan.
  • Law adoptedIndustrial areas

    Fold the scattered IND A, IND B and IND C properties into a Light Industrial (LI) district with new dimensional rules.

    Plan, page 193

    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1013: Rezoned properties to Light Industrial (LI). The resolution says it carries out the plan.
    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1018: Removed the Industrial B (IND B) district. The resolution says it carries out the plan.
    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1026: Amended the Light Industrial (LI) district. The resolution says it carries out the plan.
    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1022: Adopted the LI table of dimensional regulations. The resolution says it carries out the plan.
    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1027: Amended the Industrial C (IND C) district. The resolution says it carries out the plan.
    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1017: Amended permitted uses in the Planned Industrial Park (PIP) district. The resolution says it carries out the plan.
  • Law adoptedIndustrial areas

    Allow vertical farming in all industrial districts.

    “Vertical farming should be a permitted use in all industrial districts.”

    Plan, page 199

    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1025: Adopted the industrial use table; the table proposed in November 2024 (Resolution 2024-889) marked vertical farming permitted in LI and CI. The resolution says it carries out the plan.
    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1021: Added a definition of vertical farming to the zoning code. The resolution says it carries out the plan.
  • Law adoptedCommercial areas and hamlets

    Rezone Industrial B land at the Calverton hamlet center to a hamlet commercial district for small-scale shops.

    Plan, page 191

    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1015: Rezoned properties to the Hamlet Center (HC) district. The resolution says it carries out the plan.
  • Law adoptedCommercial areas and hamlets

    Other map changes in the plan's zoning approach, including land moved to residential and open-space districts.

    Plan, page 191

    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1011: Rezoned properties to Residence B-40 (RB40). The resolution says it carries out the plan.
    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1012: Rezoned properties to Open Space Conservation (OSC). The resolution says it carries out the plan.
    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1023: Amended the commercial districts table of dimensional regulations. The resolution says it carries out the plan.
    • April 21, 2026, Resolution 2026-406: Set a hearing on further changes to the commercial dimensional table, 4 to 1 (Halpin voting no); no adoption found.
  • Law adoptedTransfer of development rights

    Add new TDR sending and receiving areas, worked out with the TDR Committee.

    “Meetings were held with the TDR Committee to identify additions to the TDR sending and receiving areas.”

    Plan, page 204

    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1010: Amended the Transfer of Development Rights map. The resolution says it carries out the plan.
  • Law adoptedTransfer of development rights

    Change the transfer formula so one credit buys more than one small or age-restricted unit, to raise what credits are worth.

    “The current transfer formula is a one-to-one basis, meaning that one TDR credit is equal to one residential unit in a receiving district regardless of unit size or type.”

    Plan, page 203

    • December 17, 2024, Resolution 2024-1020: Amended the TDR rules, sections 301-204 through 301-208. The resolution says it carries out the plan.
    • January 6, 2026, Resolution 2026-50: Set a hearing on another TDR amendment, which would let credits be used for golf cottages; no adoption found.

Likely concerns for residents

  • The industrial and TDR changes, which most affect what can be built in Calverton, moved fastest. Several housing and design recommendations, such as the downtown cap study, the Pattern Book and the hospital district, show no action two years on.
  • Some later laws touch topics the plan covers (accessory dwelling units, the outlet district, the Community Benefit District) but do not say they carry it out. Residents cannot tell from the resolution alone whether they follow the plan’s advice.
  • The plan says “The Town should continue to monitor progress of recommendations to ensure the plan’s objectives are being met.” This site found no progress report on the plan among the Town Board’s resolutions.

How this was checked

The 25 items are the main recommendations in the plan’s land use chapter (Chapter 13), plus a few from other chapters that call for a specific law or plan. The plan has many more goals. Each item quotes the plan and links to its page.

Records searched: Town Board resolutions from September 4, 2024 to November 5, 2026: agendas and minutes from CivicClerk for 2024, and this site's meeting records and agenda packets for 2025 and 2026. Planning Board actions, building permits and capital projects were not searched.

Source: Town of Riverhead 2024 Comprehensive Plan Update (Town of Riverhead, PDF). Also see the Community Housing Plan and Preserved Land pages.
