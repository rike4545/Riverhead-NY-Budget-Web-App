What changes most?

The Tentative raises town-wide appropriations $3,294,272 (+4.23%) and the town-wide property-tax levy $1,703,910 (+2.79%).

Not counting transfers between funds, it spends $116,864,850, up 3.7% from 2026 (+$4,142,096). Counting all 19 funds, it appropriates $120,958,878, down 0.1% from 2026. The Debt Service Fund, paid for by transfers from the other funds, falls $4,294,122.

Is it within the tax cap?

It proposes a town-wide tax levy of $62,882,202, up 2.79% from 2026, for the General Fund, Highway and Street Lighting. With the special districts, the levy is $67,159,863, up 2.78% from 2026 and $1,807,211 less than this site’s forecast of $68,967,074. The Supervisor’s letter says every taxing district is within the tax cap limit of 2.79%, and no district’s levy rises by more than that.

What should I be careful not to confuse?

A Tentative is a proposal, not the budget. In 3 of the last 5 years the adopted budget matched it fund for fund; the most it moved was $743,900 of appropriations, in 2023.

Appropriations are spending authority, not proof that cash was spent. The Town levy is only the Town's property-tax share. And the Town rate shown here is not your complete property-tax bill.
