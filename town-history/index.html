Supervisors & Council Members, 2004–2026

Who has held Riverhead's top elected offices over roughly the last two decades — sourced from RiverheadLOCAL, Riverhead News-Review, and campaign/election records.

Independent project·Updated October 9, 2026·Current·Check data freshness →
This isn't a complete historical record. This is not a complete roster back to the Town's 1792 founding — the town's own Historian's office cites Daniel Well as its first Supervisor, elected at the town's first meeting in April 1792, and Sylvester Miller as its longest-serving (1841–1861), but a full year-by-year record isn't published online. This page covers what could be sourced for the last ~20 years. The Town Board always has a Supervisor plus four council seats; coverage is complete for every seat from 2016 onward, but thinner for 2008–2016, when not every seat-holder could be pinned down with a specific source. If you can source a correction or fill a gap, please flag it.

Town Supervisor

Jerome Halpin· D
Jan 2026 – Present

Currently serving. A pastor of North Shore Christian Church for about 22 years and a political newcomer before winning the Supervisor race by 37 votes.

Sources: RiverheadLOCAL, “Pastor Jerry Halpin will be sworn in as town supervisor” (Dec. 31, 2025).

Tim Hubbard· R
Jan 2024 – Jan 2026

The Town's 64th Supervisor. Previously an 8-year Town Councilman (2016–2023). Lost the November 2025 election to Jerry Halpin by 37 votes after a recount.

Sources: RiverheadLOCAL, “A new day in Riverhead: Tim Hubbard… sworn in as the county seat's 64th town supervisor” (Jan. 2, 2024). · East End Beacon, “With Recount Complete, Riverhead Town Supervisor Tim Hubbard Concedes.”

Yvette Aguiar· R
Jan 2020 – Jan 2024

The Town's 63rd Supervisor. Defeated incumbent Laura Jens-Smith in November 2019, defeated a challenge from councilwoman Catherine Kent in 2021, and did not seek a third term — her time in office ended December 31, 2023.

Sources: RiverheadLOCAL, “Riverhead supervisor bows out: Aguiar announces she will not seek a third term of office” (Feb. 2023). · Riverhead News-Review, “Outgoing Riverhead Town Supervisor Yvette Aguiar reflects on her tenure” (Dec. 2023).

Laura Jens-Smith· D
Jan 2018 – Jan 2020

The first woman elected Riverhead Town Supervisor in the town's 227-year history at the time. Defeated by Yvette Aguiar in the November 2019 election.

Sources: RiverheadLOCAL, “History is made in Riverhead: Jens-Smith sworn in as first woman town supervisor” (Jan. 1, 2018).

Sean Walter· R
Jan 2010 – Jan 2018

Elected four times, serving 2010–2017; defeated by Laura Jens-Smith in the November 2017 election. Later elected Riverhead Town Justice in 2020.

Sources: Riverhead News-Review, “Q&A with outgoing Riverhead Town supervisor Sean Walter” (Dec. 2017). · RiverheadLOCAL, “Sean Walter sworn in as Riverhead town justice” (Nov. 2020).

Phil Cardinale
Jan 2004 – Jan 2010

Defeated by Sean Walter in the November 2009 election.

Sources: Riverhead News-Review, “Former Riverhead supervisor Phil Cardinale hints at state run” (March 2012).

Town Council

Why this list looks incomplete for older years

The Town Board always has four council seats at once, but only the seat-holders who could be confirmed with a specific, sourced date are listed here — mostly from the mid-2000s onward. Current members show only their first-elected date; see the Council Scorecard for their current term.
Denise Merrifield· R
Jan 2024 – Present

Elected November 2023. Currently serving.

Sources: Riverhead News-Review, “Election 2023: Meet the candidates for Town Council” (Oct. 2023).

Joann Waski· R
Jan 2024 – Present

Elected November 2023. Currently serving.

Sources: Riverhead News-Review, “Election 2023: Meet the candidates for Town Council” (Oct. 2023).

Robert "Bob" Kern· R
Jan 2022 – Present

Elected November 2021. Currently serving.

Sources: Riverhead News-Review / Patch candidate profiles — Bob Kern (2021, 2025).

Kenneth Rothwell· R
Jan 2021 – Present

Appointed in January 2021 to fill the vacancy left by Jodi Giglio's move to the Assembly, then won his first full term in November 2021. Currently serving.

Sources: RiverheadLOCAL, “Meet Riverhead Town's new councilman, Kenneth Rothwell” (Jan. 2021).

Frank Beyrodt· R
Jan 2020 – Jan 2024

Lost a close race for council in 2017, then won a seat in November 2019. Served one term and did not seek re-election in 2023.

Sources: Riverhead News-Review, “Endorsement: Frank Beyrodt, Diane Tucci for Town Board” (Nov. 2019). · RiverheadLOCAL, “Board members, staff and residents bid farewell to three elected officials at final Town Board meeting of 2023” (Dec. 2023).

Catherine Kent· D
Jan 2018 – Jan 2022

A retired teacher, elected councilwoman in November 2017 by a narrow margin over Frank Beyrodt. Served one term, then unsuccessfully challenged incumbent Yvette Aguiar for Town Supervisor in November 2021.

Sources: Riverhead News-Review, “Live Riverhead town election results” (Nov. 2017) — Kent narrowly edges Beyrodt for the second council seat. · Riverhead News-Review, “Kent to oppose Aguiar for Town Supervisor” (Feb. 2021).

Tim Hubbard· R
Jan 2016 – Jan 2024

Elected Town Supervisor in November 2023, taking office January 2024 (see Supervisors above).

Sources: Riverhead News-Review, “Hulse, Hubbard sworn into office in town ceremony” (Jan. 2016).

Jodi Giglio· R
Jan 2010 – Jan 2021

Elected in 2009 and re-elected in 2013, serving about a decade on the Town Board before resigning to take office in the NY State Assembly (District 2), sworn in January 5, 2021.

Sources: RiverheadLOCAL, “Jodi Giglio sworn in as member of State Assembly” (Jan. 6, 2021). · New York State Assembly, Jodi Giglio member biography.

George Gabrielsen· R
Nov 2009 – Nov 2015

Elected in a November 2009 special election to fill the remaining term of a departed councilman, then won a full four-year term in 2011. Did not seek re-election in 2015, citing business commitments.

Sources: Riverhead News-Review, “Gabrielsen will not seek re-election, cites business commitments” (April 2015).

James M. Wooten· R
Jan 2008 – Jan 2020

Elected in November 2007. Served three four-year terms (12 years total) before being term-limited. Later elected Riverhead Town Clerk in 2023.

Sources: Riverhead News-Review, “Town Board members thank Wooten for his 12 years of service” (Dec. 2019). · RiverheadLOCAL, “Former councilman James Wooten returns to Riverhead Town Hall” (Dec. 3, 2020).
