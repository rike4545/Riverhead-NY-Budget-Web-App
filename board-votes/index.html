Board votes

How each Town Board member voted when the Board did not agree, and how often each pair voted alike.

Independent, unofficial project·Data last refreshed November 11, 2026·Recently refreshed·See data update details →

In plain English

From January 7, 2025 to November 5, 2026 the Board voted on 2,029 resolutions at 49 meetings, and did not agree on 39 of them. On those, Kenneth Rothwell and Joann Waski voted alike most often (33 of 39, 85%); Jerry Halpin and Kenneth Rothwell least often (3 of 19, 16%).
  • Contested: a resolution where the members present did not all cast the same vote: someone voted no or abstained while others voted yes.
  • Abstaining: a member may abstain because of a conflict, such as a vote on their own appointment or pay. An abstention is not always a disagreement.
  • Absences: are not counted as disagreement. They are in each member’s totals below.

Sources and method

The site's voting record, read from the Town's meeting minutes and agenda packets on CivicClerk; see Town Board meetings. The site holds every meeting from January 7, 2025; earlier years have only one meeting each, so they are left out. Built by etl/build_vote_agreement.py. Patterns in votes are not evidence of why a member voted as they did.
