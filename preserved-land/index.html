Preserved land

Every Riverhead farm whose development rights have been bought or restricted, mapped from Suffolk County's records: who holds the rights, how many acres, and when.

Independent, unofficial project·Data last refreshed November 10, 2026·Recently refreshed·See data update details →

In plain English

The County's records show 8,581 acres of preserved farmland in Riverhead, on 257 parcels: about 20% of the Town's 43,809 acres. The County holds the rights to 5,961 acres, the Town to 1,546, and the two together to 435.
  • Development rights: when a town, the County or the State buys a farm’s development rights, the owner keeps the land and can farm it, but it can never be built on.
  • Who pays: the Town buys development rights through its Community Preservation Fund, the 2% tax on real-estate sales; the County and State have their own programs.
  • What is not here: parks and open space the Town owns outright, which the County keeps in other layers. This page is the farmland.

Sources and method

  • Parcels: Suffolk County GIS, Farmland Development Rights (FarmlandPDRPolygon), last updated by the County February 13, 2025. Riverhead's are the parcels whose County tax-map number starts with the Town's district, 0600. Acres and the holder of the rights are the County's. Street addresses and tax-map numbers are left out. Refreshed by etl/build_preserved_land.py; data last changed November 7, 2026.
  • The Town's area is measured from the County Board of Elections' district map, as on the Election map, so the share preserved is approximate.
  • The Community Preservation Fund has bought $76,983,250 of land and rights since 1999, by its 2025 financial statement. See the Community Preservation Fund and the regional CPF counsel pages.
