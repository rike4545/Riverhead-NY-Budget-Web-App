Data Downloads

Every dataset on this site, free to download as CSV (for spreadsheets) or JSON (for developers). All figures trace back to official Town documents.

Independent project·Updated October 10, 2026·Current·Check data freshness →

In plain English

Working on a story, a study, or your own analysis? Take the data with you — no scraping required.
  • CSV: opens directly in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers.
  • JSON: the same data in the structured format this site runs on.
  • Attribution: data is derived from public Town of Riverhead records; verify against the official documents before publishing.
