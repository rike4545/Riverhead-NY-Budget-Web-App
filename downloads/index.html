Data Downloads

Every dataset on this site, free to download as CSV (for spreadsheets) or JSON (for developers). All figures trace back to official Town documents.

Independent, unofficial project·Data last refreshed October 31, 2026·Recently refreshed·See data update details →

In plain English

Working on a story, a study, or your own analysis? Take the data with you — no scraping required.
  • CSV: opens directly in Excel, Google Sheets, or Numbers.
  • JSON: the same data in the structured format this site runs on.
  • Attribution: data is derived from public Town of Riverhead records; verify against the official documents before publishing.

Independent public-records guide for Riverhead residents.

Riverhead Budget Live is an independent project. It is not run, approved, or endorsed by the Town of Riverhead. The site organizes public information and provides analysis; it is not a substitute for the Town’s official records. Check the linked source documents before relying on an important figure. Basic, aggregated site-usage statistics are collected through Google Analytics. You do not need an account to use this site.
