Supervisor’s office pay compared

The 2026 salaries of the five roles in the Riverhead Supervisor’s office, beside the same roles in Southampton, Southold, Smithtown and Huntington, from each town’s own records.

Independent, unofficial project·Data last refreshed November 11, 2026·Recently refreshed·See data update details →

In plain English

Every Riverhead figure is from the Town’s 2026 salary schedule in the January 6, 2026 agenda packet. Each other town’s figures are from its own 2026 records: an adopted budget, a salary chart or the salary resolutions of its organizational meeting, each linked below. Towns organize the Supervisor’s office differently, so a lower or higher salary can reflect a different job rather than different pay for the same one. For Riverhead’s other management salaries, see Management pay and benefits.
  • Five roles, four people: Riverhead’s Chief of Staff is also its Town Budget Officer, paid one salary.
  • Base salary: pay before benefits, longevity and overtime. The towns’ records differ in what else they list.
  • Counterpart: the title doing the same job in another town. Where a town has none, the table says so.

What the comparison shows

  • Supervisor. At $110,000, Riverhead’s Supervisor is paid less than in any of the 4 other towns: Southampton’s $149,773 (Riverhead 27% less), Southold’s $129,502 (Riverhead 15% less), Smithtown’s $161,694 (Riverhead 32% less) and Huntington’s $162,903 (Riverhead 33% less). The $118,919 the budget set aside would also have been the lowest. Riverhead is the second smallest of the five by population; against its residents, Southampton 2.0 times as many, Southold 67% as many, Smithtown 3.2 times as many and Huntington 5.7 times as many.
  • Deputy Supervisor. Towns use the job differently. Riverhead’s deputy serves full time at $105,527, 96% of the Supervisor’s salary. Southampton also pays a full salary ($99,246, 66% of its Supervisor’s) and Huntington also pays a full salary ($162,546, 100% of its Supervisor’s). Southold pays $5,000 and Smithtown pays $35,000 for the year, so there it is not a full-time salary; Smithtown’s deputy is a Town Board member.
  • Chief of Staff and Budget Officer. None of the 4 other towns’ records lists a chief of staff. For budget work, Southampton has a Town Comptroller ($166,956, outside the Supervisor’s office), Southold has a Town Comptroller ($166,464, outside the Supervisor’s office), Smithtown has a Town Budget Officer ($168,689, outside the Supervisor’s office) and Huntington has a Director of Finance ($156,000, in the Supervisor’s office). Riverhead’s combined role is paid $80,567, less than any of them. Smithtown’s Town Budget Officer has the same title as Riverhead’s, at $168,689, though in its Comptroller’s department. The Comptrollers run whole finance departments, a larger job than Riverhead’s role.
  • Legislative Aide or Secretary. Riverhead’s $59,740 compares with Southampton’s Special Assistant (Confidential Secretary) - Supervisor at $105,033, Southold’s Confidential Secretary, Office of the Supervisor at $97,671, Smithtown’s Secretary to Supervisor at $118,083 and Huntington’s Confidential Secretary at $54,667. Riverhead’s aide was appointed in January 2026, and pay for these posts usually rises with years of service (Southold’s resolution adds longevity pay to both of its secretaries), so part of any gap may be tenure rather than the job.

This is a starting point, not a pay study. A full one would match duties, hours, tenure and benefits. Larger towns run larger offices: the biggest here has 5.7 times Riverhead’s residents.

Towns not included, and why

  • Brookhaven. Its 2026 adopted budget could not be read: the town's website refused this site's automated requests, and the latest budget found through search is 2022.
  • Islip. Its 2026 adopted budget is a 90 MB file on a town website that refused this site's automated requests.
  • Babylon. Its 2026 salaries are set in administrative salary plan schedules by level and step, attached to Board resolutions, rather than listed by title; they were not read.
  • East Hampton. Its 2026 adopted budget is posted on the town's website, which refused this site's automated requests.
  • Shelter Island. Its January 5, 2026 resolutions appoint a deputy supervisor and a confidential secretary to be paid 'as per the 2026 budget' without stating amounts, and the budget was not found.
